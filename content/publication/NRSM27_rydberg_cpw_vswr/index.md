abstract: "Rydberg atom electrometry relates the field at the atoms to the Autler–Townes splitting of an electromagnetically induced transparency (EIT) resonance through fundamental constants and a calculable dipole moment, removing the dependence on a probe calibrated against a field established by other means. Previous near-field mapping and in-cell imaging work reports the field distribution itself; this work extends the technique to a derived transmission-line parameter, the voltage standing wave ratio (VSWR), of a coplanar waveguide (CPW). A CPW board driven at 12.010 GHz is interrogated by a 20 mm cylindrical rubidium vapour cell, held 7.7 mm above the conductor plane and translated along the line, and the standing-wave envelope is fitted for matched 50 Ω load and short-circuit terminations. Overall, the measurement demonstrates non-contact evaluation of a derived parameter of a planar transmission line, with the field referred to atomic structure rather than to a calibrated probe. A combined standard uncertainty for this geometry has not yet been established."
